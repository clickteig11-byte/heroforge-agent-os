# 01 — The Minimalist Hero Restraint Law (Iterative Critique Loop)

Linked to: [[00-HeroForge-MOC]] · [[02-Seven-Flagship-Replication-Prompts]]

## Why Our Initial Heroes Graded at `B` and `B-`
During our Visual QA critique loop, we identified the exact anti-patterns that make AI-generated heroes feel templated or over-designed:
1. **Competing UI Clutter**: Having a floating nav, a bordered badge pill with a dot, two side-by-side buttons, AND a 4-cell stat card or large selector box crowds the photograph.
2. **Decorative Interface Jargon**: Labels like `"Practice Bento"`, `"Lithic Archive"`, `"3D Calibre Vitrine"`, and `"Switch Full-Bleed Residence Plate"` read like interface decoration rather than clear human navigation.
3. **Orphaned Headline Words**: Letting `"light."` wrap onto its own third line weakens typographic authority and covers too much of the background image.
4. **Workspace Color Leakage**: Using the workspace's light-blue (`#38BDF8`) accent on horology or architecture showcase sites breaks material authenticity.

---

## The A+ Minimalist Hero Contract (Baked Into All Skills)
- **Navigation**: Floating pill bar with **short, clear 1-word names** (`Projects`, `Studio`, `Materials`, `Inquire` or `Movement`, `Craft`, `Specifications`, `Inquire`).
- **One Small Project Label**: Unboxed, tracked monospace (`01 / GRINDAVÍK, ICELAND` or `CALIBRE 01 · GENEVA`).
- **Two Intentional Headline Lines**: Explicit line break + `text-wrap: balance` so every line is deliberate and compact.
- **One Concise Sentence (`<= 38ch`)**: Comfortable reading width that leaves `70%+` of the hero photograph unobstructed.
- **One Primary CTA Pill**: Never place a second ghost button beside the primary hero action.
- **One Quiet Supporting Element**: Either a compact inline pill switcher (`01 Reykjanes · 02 Naoshima · 03 Atacama`) or a single-line baseline spec (`312 Parts · 96h Reserve · Raw Maillechort`).
