## 1. Why We Consolidated
Previously, creative rules were fragmented across 36 specialized skill files (`hero-design`, `typography-director`, `creative-director`, `deslopify`, etc.). After grading our initial Watch (`B-`) and Architecture (`B`) heroes, we codified the **Minimalist Hero Restraint Law** into a unified master skill:
- **`heroforge-master-studio/SKILL.md`**: Single source of truth for Hero Restraint, Intentional 2-Line Typography Wrapping, Natural 1-Word Navigation Labels, Subject Dominance (`>= 65%` unobstructed photo/3D canvas), and Workspace vs. Showcase Color Isolation.
- **`hyperframes-motion-graphics/SKILL.md`**: Deterministic 60-second (1-minute) HTML-to-MP4 X-Post motion-graphics choreography using `window.__renderTime` and `ffmpeg`.
